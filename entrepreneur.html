Foundation

Before the boardroom

  • B.A., Criminal Justice, minor in Political Science, Indiana University Bloomington (1989)
  • Doctor of Jurisprudence, Indiana University Maurer School of Law (1993), focused on litigation, social justice and political law
  • Associate at Dinsmore & Shohl LLP, defending Fortune 500 companies in complex products liability, insurance and medical malpractice cases
  • Director of Legal Affairs and Risk Management, Meharry Medical College (1996 to 1999)
  • Adjunct professor of law at Tennessee State University and Fisk University (1996 to 2001)
Working for the people

Private sector record

  • Built the largest Black-owned government relations firm in Tennessee after coming off the Metro Council
  • Helped raise over $500K for the National Museum of African American Music
  • Established The Shirley Ann Coates Scholarship for Tennessee State University students
  • Helped raise over $80K for the Northwest YMCA as chair of major gifts
  • As Board Chair of Jefferson Street United Merchants Partnership, secured nearly $1M for the Gateway to Heritage beautification project
  • Created the partnership between Nashville General Hospital and MDHA that provided COVID testing to 5,000+ residents
  • Supported the establishment of Oracle's new Nashville headquarters
